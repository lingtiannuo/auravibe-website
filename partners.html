Host with us和我们一起办

We partner with venues, brands and communities who want to be part of a great event, not just sponsor it.我们想携手的，是愿意真正参与进来、一起把活动办好的场地、品牌和社群，而不只是挂个赞助名。

Venues场地

Rooftops, restaurants, galleries and studios. We bring a curated, 21+ crowd and run the whole event end to end.屋顶、餐厅、画廊和工作室。我们带来精选的 21 岁以上宾客，并负责整场活动的运营。

Brands品牌

Product moments that fit the moment, not a booth in the corner. Tastings, workshops, gifts that people keep.融入活动本身的品牌体验，而不是角落里的展台。品鉴、工作坊、让人愿意留着的小礼物。

Communities社群
