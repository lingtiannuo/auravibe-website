Privacy隐私政策

The short version: we ask for a lot so every room is real, and we use it for nothing else.简单来说：我们收集这些信息，只是为了保证每一场的人都真实可信，不会用作其他用途。

01

What we collect我们收集什么

Everything you put in your application: your answers, contact details, LinkedIn and Instagram links, and the photos you upload.

你在申请中填写的所有内容：问卷回答、联系方式、LinkedIn 和 Instagram 链接，以及你上传的照片。

02

Why we use it我们用来做什么

  • To review your application and build guest lists.
  • To confirm your identity and check you in at the door.
  • To contact you about nights you applied for, and, if you opted in, about future events.
  • To turn your prompt answers into icebreaker games at events you attend.
  • 审核申请、安排每场活动的宾客名单。
  • 核实身份，并在入场时核对。
  • 就你申请的活动联系你；如果你选择了接收通知，也会告诉你之后的活动。
  • 把你的话题回答变成你所参加活动的破冰游戏素材。
03

Who sees it谁能看到

Only the AuraVibe team. Your photos are never posted publicly or shown to other guests. We don't sell your information to anyone. Your prompt answers may be read out or shown during icebreakers at events you attend.

只有 AuraVibe 团队。你的照片绝不会公开发布或展示给其他嘉宾。我们不会把你的信息卖给任何人。你的话题回答可能会在你所参加活动的破冰环节中被读出或展示。

04

Event photos活动照片

We only feature you in photos or videos on social media if you said yes on your application. You can change your mind at any time by emailing us.

只有你在申请中选择了“愿意”，我们才会在社交媒体上发布有你的照片或视频。你可以随时发邮件改变主意。

[我们会在你最后一次参加活动后保存申请资料 XX 个月，之后删除。]

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time. Email [privacy@yourdomain.com] and we'll reply within [X] days.
