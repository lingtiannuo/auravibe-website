Code of conduct行为准则

AuraVibe works because everyone in the room chose to be there for the same reason. These rules protect that.AuraVibe 之所以成立，是因为现场每个人都怀着同样的目的而来。这些准则就是为了守护这一点。

01

Come for connection为交朋友而来

Everyone in the room applied for the same reason: to meet people. Please don't sell, recruit, pitch a business, or promote your own events or services. Swapping contacts because you hit it off is the whole point; working the room is not.

现场每个人申请的理由都一样：认识新朋友。请不要推销、拉人、推广生意，或宣传你自己的活动和服务。聊得投机互加联系方式正是我们想看到的，但“扫场拓客”不行。

02

Respect and consent尊重与同意

A no is a full answer, whether it's to a drink, a dance, a photo or a follow-up message. No unwanted touching, comments or attention, at the event or after it.

无论是邀请喝一杯、跳支舞、拍张照，还是活动后的私信，对方说“不”就是答案。活动中和活动后，都不允许不受欢迎的触碰、评论或纠缠。

03

Be who you said you are做申请里的那个你

Same name, same face, same person as your application. Your ID has to match at check-in, and your spot can't be passed to someone else.

名字、长相都要和申请一致。入场时证件需与申请信息相符，名额不能转让给他人。

04

Drink like an adult理性饮酒

Some events serve alcohol. Know your limit. Our hosts may ask anyone who is too drunk to look after themselves, or others, to head home.

部分活动会提供酒精饮料，请把握好分寸。如果有人醉到照顾不了自己或影响他人，主持人可能会请对方先离开。

05

Protect each other's privacy保护彼此的隐私

Ask before you post a photo of another guest. Don't share anyone's name, workplace or contact details without their permission.

发布其他嘉宾的照片前请先征得同意。未经允许，不要透露任何人的名字、公司或联系方式。

06

Zero tolerance零容忍

Harassment, discrimination or hate of any kind means you'll be asked to leave and won't be invited back.

任何形式的骚扰、歧视或仇恨言行，都会被请离现场，并且不会再收到邀请。

07

Speak up说出来

If something feels off, tell any host at the event or email auravibenyc@gmail.com. Every report is taken seriously and kept confidential.

如果感觉不对劲，请直接告诉现场任何一位主持人，或发邮件到 auravibenyc@gmail.com。每一次反馈我们都会认真对待并严格保密。
