Applying申请

Who can apply?谁可以申请？

Anyone 21 or older who lives in or around New York. You'll need a valid photo ID at the door.年满 21 岁、住在纽约或周边的人都可以申请。入场时需要出示有效的带照片证件。

Why do you ask for photos and LinkedIn?为什么需要照片和 LinkedIn？

To keep every room real. We use them to confirm you are who you say you are and to check you in. They are never posted or shown to other guests.为了保证现场每个人都真实可信。我们只用它们核实身份和入场核对，绝不会公开，也不会给其他嘉宾看。

How do you choose guests?你们怎么挑选嘉宾？

Someone on our team reads every application and builds each guest list so the room works together: a good mix of interests, backgrounds and personalities. Not getting a spot one night doesn't mean you won't get the next.团队成员会逐一阅读申请，让每一场的人彼此聊得来：兴趣、背景和性格都有好的搭配。这一场没排上，不代表下一场也不行。

Do I need to apply for every night?每场活动都要重新申请吗？

No. Your application stays on file and we invite you to nights that match what you’re into. Update it any time by emailing us.不用。你的申请会一直保留，我们会邀请你参加符合你兴趣的活动。资料有变化可以随时发邮件告诉我们。

At the night活动当天

Is this a dating event?这是相亲活动吗？

Mostly no. AuraVibe is about friends and community. Nights that are for singles only, like speed dating, are clearly labeled.大部分不是。AuraVibe 主要是交朋友、拓展圈子。只限单身参加的活动（比如快速约会）会提前明确标注。

Can I come with a friend?可以和朋友一起来吗？

Yes. Ask them to apply too and name each other under “How did you hear about AuraVibe?”. We'll try to put you on the same list.可以。请朋友也提交申请，并在“你是从哪里知道 AuraVibe 的”里写上彼此的名字，我们会尽量把你们安排在同一场。

Do I need to know anyone?需要认识里面的人吗？

No. Every night is designed for people who come on their own. Hosts make the first introductions.不需要。每一场都是为一个人来的嘉宾设计的，主持人会帮你开个头。

What should I wear?应该穿什么？

Each invite comes with a dress code. Usually it’s “dressed-up casual”.每封邀请函都会写着装要求，一般是“精致休闲”。

Safety & privacy安全与隐私

Will I end up on social media?我会出现在社交媒体上吗？

Only if you said yes on your application. Otherwise we keep you out of anything we post.只有你在申请里选择了“愿意”才会。否则我们发布的内容里不会出现你。

How do you use my information?你们怎么使用我的信息？

Only to review your application, check you in and contact you about nights. Never sold, never posted. See our privacy page for details.只用于审核申请、入场核对和活动通知，绝不出售、绝不公开。详见隐私政策页面。

What if someone makes me uncomfortable?如果有人让我不舒服怎么办？

Tell any host right away or email [safety@yourdomain.com]. Every report is taken seriously and kept confidential.请立刻告诉任何一位主持人，或发邮件到 [safety@yourdomain.com]。每一次反馈我们都会认真处理并严格保密。
