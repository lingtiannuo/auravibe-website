How it works参加流程

No swiping, no cold DMs. One application, then we do the matchmaking for the room.不用滑卡，不用尬聊私信。提交一次申请，剩下的组局交给我们。

  1. 01

    Apply提交申请

    About 8 minutes and three recent photos. Tell us what you're into and who you want to meet.大约 8 分钟，上传 3 张近照。告诉我们你喜欢什么、想认识什么样的人。

  2. 02

    We build the room我们来组局

    A real person reads every application and balances each guest list by interests, background and energy.每份申请都由真人阅读，再根据兴趣、背景和性格，搭配每一场的宾客名单。

  3. 03

    Check your inbox查看邮件

    You'll hear back by email within 3 days. If you're in, the invite has the date, time and ticket link.3 天内你会收到邮件。入选的话，邀请函里有日期、时间和购票链接。

  4. 04

    Show up准时到场

    Bring a photo ID. The address lands the day before, and the icebreakers are built from your own answers.带上有照片的证件。地址会在活动前一天发给你，现场的破冰游戏就取材于你的申请回答。

Nine kinds of nights九种夜晚

Tell us which ones you like on your application and we'll invite you to those first.在申请里告诉我们你喜欢哪几种，我们会优先邀请你参加。

01

Rooftop parties屋顶派对

Golden hour, the skyline and a DJ who reads the room.黄金时刻、城市天际线，还有一个懂气氛的 DJ。

02

Dinners晚宴

One long table of strangers and courses paced for talking.一张坐满陌生人的长桌，上菜节奏刚好留给聊天。

03

After-hours shows, with someone to talk about them with.闭馆后的展览，看完有人一起聊。

05

Speed friending快速交友

Short rounds, good prompts, no awkward silences.短轮次、好话题，不冷场。

06

Singles only, clearly labeled, matched with care.仅限单身，提前标明，用心匹配。

Board games, drinks and a little friendly competition.桌游、饮料，加一点友好的胜负欲。

Real people, verified.真实的人，经过核实。

Every guest is checked against LinkedIn and recent photos before they're invited.每位嘉宾受邀前，我们都会通过 LinkedIn 和近照核实身份。

Who can apply?谁可以申请？

Anyone 21 or older who lives in or around New York. You'll need a valid photo ID at the door.年满 21 岁、住在纽约或周边的人都可以申请。入场时需要出示有效的带照片证件。

Why do you ask for photos and LinkedIn?为什么需要照片和 LinkedIn？

To keep every room real. We use them to confirm you are who you say you are and to check you in. They are never posted or shown to other guests.为了保证现场每个人都真实可信。我们只用它们核实身份和入场核对，绝不会公开，也不会给其他嘉宾看。

How do you choose guests?你们怎么挑选嘉宾？

Someone on our team reads every application and builds each guest list so the room works together: a good mix of interests, backgrounds and personalities. Not getting a spot one night doesn't mean you won't get the next.团队成员会逐一阅读申请，让每一场的人彼此聊得来：兴趣、背景和性格都有好的搭配。这一场没排上，不代表下一场也不行。

How much does it cost?费用是多少？

Each night is ticketed separately and the price is on your invite. [Typical range: $XX–$XX]每场活动单独售票，价格写在邀请函里。[常见价格：$XX–$XX]

Is this a dating event?这是相亲活动吗？

Mostly no. AuraVibe is about friends and community. Nights that are for singles only, like speed dating, are clearly labeled.大部分不是。AuraVibe 主要是交朋友、拓展圈子。只限单身参加的活动（比如快速约会）会提前明确标注。
