About AuraVibe关于 AuraVibe

The room we wished existed我们想要的那个场子

AuraVibe is a curated social club for young New Yorkers who want a bigger circle outside of work. We host small nights designed for meeting people you actually click with.AuraVibe 是一个精选社交俱乐部，面向想在工作之余扩大圈子的纽约年轻人。我们办小规模的活动，专门为了让你遇见灵魂契合的人。

Why we started为什么做 AuraVibe

A lot of New Yorkers know the feeling: a full calendar, and not enough people who really get you.很多纽约人都有这种感觉：日程排得满满当当，真正懂你的人却没几个。

Work friends stay at work. Apps feel like a second job. Parties are fun, but you leave without anyone’s name.同事只是同事，交友软件像第二份工作，派对很热闹，但散场时你一个名字都没记住。

So we started hosting the nights we wanted to go to: small enough to talk, curated enough that the person next to you is worth meeting, and structured just enough that nobody ends up alone by the bar.所以我们开始办自己也想参加的活动：规模小到能好好聊天，筛选用心到身边每个人都值得认识，再加一点恰到好处的设计，让没有人独自站在吧台边。

What we care about我们在意的

01

Real over polished真实，胜过精致

Real names, real photos, real conversations. We verify every guest so you can relax.真名、真照片、真对话。每位嘉宾都经过核实，你可以放心交流。

02

Small rooms小而精

We keep every night small enough that you can actually meet the people you want to.每一场都控制规模，让你真的能认识想认识的人。

03

Mixed on purpose刻意混搭

Different industries, hometowns and personalities. The best conversations happen across lines.不同行业、不同家乡、不同性格。最好的对话，往往发生在圈子的交界处。

04

Care in the details在意细节

Hosts on the floor, icebreakers that aren’t cringe, and a code of conduct we actually enforce.现场有主持人照应，破冰游戏不尴尬，行为准则说到做到。

The hosts主理人

The people who read your application and say hi at the door.读你申请、在门口跟你打招呼的人。
