About AuraVibe关于 AuraVibe

The room we wished existed我们想要的那个场子

AuraVibe is a curated social club for young New Yorkers who want a bigger circle outside of work. We host small events designed for meeting people you actually click with.AuraVibe 是一个精选社交俱乐部，面向想在工作之余扩大圈子的纽约年轻人。我们办小规模的活动，专门为了让你遇见灵魂契合的人。

Why AuraVibeWhy AURAVIBE

You’ve probably had this moment too.你大概也有过这种时刻：

Your contacts keep growing, but only a few people are ever free to hang out. You see the same faces every day and talk about the same things. You want to meet new people and try something new, but you don’t know where to start.通讯录越来越长，能随时约出来的人却没几个。
每天见的还是那几张脸，聊的还是那几件事。
想认识点新朋友、试点没做过的事，却不知道从哪儿开始。

We felt the same. So we built a place for it. That’s AURAVIBE.我们也一样。
所以干脆自己做了一个地方——这就是 AURAVIBE。

You don’t need to be great at small talk or especially outgoing. Coming alone is completely fine: everyone at the table started out as a stranger.在这里，你不用很会聊天，也不用多外向。
一个人来完全没问题，在座的每个人，都是从陌生开始的。

Share a meal, grab a coffee, play a game, or wander a corner of New York you’ve never been to. By the end of the night, your phone has a few new people you actually want to see again next week.一起吃顿饭、喝杯咖啡、玩局游戏，或者去纽约哪个没去过的角落走走。
散场的时候，手机里多了几个下周还想约的人。

No forced networking, no faking familiarity, and no “meet five people tonight” KPI. You’re here, so just enjoy it.不用刻意社交，不用假装熟络，
也不用给自己定什么“今晚要认识几个人”的 KPI。
来都来了，开心就好。

Come a little curious, leave a little happier.
Aura on, stress off.带着一点好奇来，带着一点开心走。
Aura on, stress off.

Founder主理人

Just the right meeting刚刚好的相逢

I’m one of the founders of AuraVibe.我是 AuraVibe 的主理人。

New York’s lights come on early, and its people move fast. After I moved here, I slowly realized this city never runs short of encounters. What it lacks is real understanding. On the subway, in cafés, in the gallery on the corner, we brush past so many people every day, yet so few stay long enough for a few honest words.纽约的灯亮得很早，人也走得很快。来到这座城市之后，我慢慢发现，这里从不缺少相遇，缺少的是相知。地铁里、咖啡馆里、街角的画廊里，每天擦肩而过的人很多，可真正能留下来，说上几句真心话的人，却少之又少。

The more people around you, the easier it is to feel alone. Not because no one is there, but because no one really gets you. So I kept wondering: what if there were a place where people could set down their titles and labels, didn’t have to please anyone or force themselves to fit in, and ended up at the same table simply because they clicked?人越多，越容易觉得孤单。不是没有人陪，而是没有人懂。于是我常常想，如果有一处地方，能让人卸下身份与标签，不必刻意迎合，也不必勉强合群，只因合拍而坐在一起，那该多好。

And so, AuraVibe.于是有了 AuraVibe。

Here we keep a corner for people 21 and over, a place to slow down. After work, have a cup of tea, make something with your hands, or just sit quietly for a while. No agenda, no pressure. Whoever you meet, it was meant to be.在这里，我们给 21 岁以上的年轻人留一个可以慢下来的角落。工作之余，喝一盏茶，做一件手作，或者只是安静地待一会儿。没有目的，没有负担，遇见谁，都是缘分。

I believe you don’t need many friends, just ones you click with. You don’t need to fit in, only to be on the same wavelength.我相信，朋友不必多，合拍就好。
不必合群，只需同频。

I hope that at AuraVibe, you find a meeting that feels just right.愿你在 AuraVibe，遇见一场刚刚好的相逢。

LumiFounder, AuraVibeAuraVibe 主理人

Founder主理人

Another home in New York在纽约的另一个家

Hi, I’m Faye, one of the founders of AuraVibe. I’d like to tell you what AuraVibe looks like in my mind.Hi，我是菲儿 Faye，AuraVibe 的主理人之一。
想跟你聊聊，我心里的 AuraVibe 是什么样子。

New York nights are always lit up. The subway is packed, weekend brunch always has a line, and it seems everyone is busy and no one is short on friends. But I know many people feel the way I do: walking down Fifth Avenue after work, or coming home alone to the apartment late at night, it suddenly hits you. This city is so lively, yet there’s no place that truly feels like yours.纽约的夜晚总是灯火通明。
地铁里挤满了人，周末的 brunch 永远要排队，好像每个人都很忙，也都不缺朋友。
可我知道，很多人和我一样，下班走在第五大道上，或者深夜一个人回到公寓，会突然觉得：这座城市很热闹，但好像没有一个地方，是真正属于自己的。

Live abroad long enough and the word “home” becomes very specific. It’s a hot meal. It’s someone remembering what was bothering you last week. It’s opening the door and hearing someone say, with a smile, “Hey, you made it.”在国外待久了，「家」这个字会变得很具体。
它是一顿热饭，是有人记得你上周说过的烦恼，是推开门的时候，有人笑着问一句「你来啦」。

So I wanted to make a place like that. It may not be for everyone, but I believe there will always be people who want to make it their other home in New York.所以我想做一个这样的地方。
它不一定适合所有人，但我相信，总会有一些人，愿意把这里当成在纽约的另一个家。

Here you can talk about the “serious” things: a hard problem at work, what’s next in your life, the book you’re reading, what you’re learning, or the thing you’ve always wanted to do but haven’t started yet.在这里，你可以聊很「正经」的事：
工作上的难题，下一步的人生规划，正在读的书、正在学的东西，或者你一直想做却还没开始的那件事。

You can also talk about the “not-so-serious” things: venting about your boss, complaining about rent, the worries and small grievances of lately. No one here knows the everyday you, so there’s no image to keep up. Say whatever you like.你也可以聊很「不正经」的事：
吐槽老板，抱怨房租，说说最近的烦恼和委屈。
反正这里没人认识平时的你，你不需要维持任何形象，想说什么就说什么。

And of course, you can bring a topic we’ve never thought of. Everyone arrives with their own aura: some like sunshine, some like neon at midnight, some as quiet as a cup of tea slowly going cool. When similar and different energies meet, there’s always a surprise.当然，你也可以带来一个我们从没想过的话题。
每个人都带着自己的 aura 来，有人像阳光，有人像深夜的霓虹，有人安静得像一杯慢慢凉下来的茶。
相似的、不一样的能量碰在一起，总会有意想不到的惊喜。

AuraVibe is free and open. You can be quiet or loud, cool or a little goofy. Being together won’t always be perfect. There will be good days and not-so-good days, and we’re ready to hold all of them.AuraVibe 很自由，也很包容。
你可以安静，也可以热闹；可以很酷，也可以有点傻气。
相处不会永远完美，可能有好，也可能有不那么好的时候，这些我们都愿意接住。

But there is one thing we insist on, our only ground rule:但有一件事，是我们唯一坚持的前提：

Sincerity, and kindness.真诚，和善良。

Bring these two things, and everything else is welcome.只要你带着这两样东西来，其余的一切，我们都欢迎。

I hope you find a little feeling of home here. And I hope that one day you’ll share your own light with the next person who walks through the door.希望你在这里，能找到一点回家的感觉。
也希望有一天，你会把自己的光，分给下一个推门进来的人。

Aura on, stress off.

FayeFounder, AuraVibeAuraVibe 主理人
