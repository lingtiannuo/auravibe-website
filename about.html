About AuraVibe关于 AuraVibe

The room we wished existed我们想要的那个场子

AuraVibe is a curated social club for young New Yorkers who want a bigger circle outside of work. We host small events designed for meeting people you actually click with.AuraVibe 是一个精选社交俱乐部，面向想在工作之余扩大圈子的纽约年轻人。我们办小规模的活动，专门为了让你遇见灵魂契合的人。

Why AuraVibeWhy AURAVIBE

You’ve probably had this moment too.你大概也有过这种时刻：

Your contacts keep growing, but only a few people are ever free to hang out. You see the same faces every day and talk about the same things. You want to meet new people and try something new, but you don’t know where to start.通讯录越来越长，能随时约出来的人却没几个。
每天见的还是那几张脸，聊的还是那几件事。
想认识点新朋友、试点没做过的事，却不知道从哪儿开始。

We felt the same. So we built a place for it. That’s AURAVIBE.我们也一样。
所以干脆自己做了一个地方——这就是 AURAVIBE。

You don’t need to be great at small talk or especially outgoing. Coming alone is completely fine: everyone at the table started out as a stranger.在这里，你不用很会聊天，也不用多外向。
一个人来完全没问题，在座的每个人，都是从陌生开始的。

Share a meal, grab a coffee, play a game, or wander a corner of New York you’ve never been to. By the end of the night, your phone has a few new people you actually want to see again next week.一起吃顿饭、喝杯咖啡、玩局游戏，或者去纽约哪个没去过的角落走走。
散场的时候，手机里多了几个下周还想约的人。

No forced networking, no faking familiarity, and no “meet five people tonight” KPI. You’re here, so just enjoy it.不用刻意社交，不用假装熟络，
也不用给自己定什么“今晚要认识几个人”的 KPI。
来都来了，开心就好。

Come a little curious, leave a little happier.
Aura on, stress off.带着一点好奇来，带着一点开心走。
Aura on, stress off.

What we care about我们在意的

01

Real over polished真实，胜过精致

Real names, real photos, real conversations. We verify every guest so you can relax.真名、真照片、真对话。每位嘉宾都经过核实，你可以放心交流。

02

Small rooms小而精

We keep every event small enough that you can actually meet the people you want to.每一场都控制规模，让你真的能认识想认识的人。

03

Mixed on purpose刻意混搭

Different industries, hometowns and personalities. The best conversations happen across lines.不同行业、不同家乡、不同性格。最好的对话，往往发生在圈子的交界处。

04

Care in the details在意细节

Hosts on the floor, icebreakers that aren’t cringe, and a code of conduct we actually enforce.现场有主持人照应，破冰游戏不尴尬，行为准则说到做到。
