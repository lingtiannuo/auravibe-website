Nine kinds of nights九种夜晚

What we host across the year. Pick your favorites on your application.我们一年中会办的活动类型。可以在申请里勾选你最喜欢的。

01

Rooftop parties屋顶派对

Golden hour, the skyline and a DJ who reads the room.黄金时刻、城市天际线，还有一个懂气氛的 DJ。

02

Dinners晚宴

One long table of strangers and courses paced for talking.一张坐满陌生人的长桌，上菜节奏刚好留给聊天。

03

Art & gallery nights艺术与画廊夜

After-hours shows, with someone to talk about them with.闭馆后的展览，看完有人一起聊。

04

Run clubs & wellness跑团与身心

Morning miles or Pilates, then coffee that runs long.晨跑或普拉提，然后是一杯越聊越久的咖啡。

05

Speed friending快速交友

Short rounds, good prompts, no awkward silences.短轮次、好话题，不冷场。

06

Speed dating快速约会

Singles only, clearly labeled, matched with care.仅限单身，提前标明，用心匹配。

07

Game nights桌游之夜

Board games, drinks and a little friendly competition.桌游、饮料，加一点友好的胜负欲。

08

Day trips一日游

Beaches, farms and small towns, an hour out of the city.出城一小时，海边、农场和小镇。

09

Workshops手作工作坊

Make something with your hands, leave with new numbers.亲手做点东西，顺便加上几个新朋友。
